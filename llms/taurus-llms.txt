# Taurus

> Taurus SA (Taurus HQ) is a Geneva-based digital asset infrastructure provider for banks and financial institutions. Its platform covers institutional custody (Taurus-PROTECT), tokenization and digital asset issuance (Taurus-CAPITAL), blockchain node and indexing services (Taurus-EXPLORER), and institutional trading connectivity over REST and FIX (Taurus-PRIME). APIs authenticate with a bearer token plus HMAC request signing, use cursor-based pagination, enforce governance rules with digital signatures, and ship first-party Java, Go, Python, and TypeScript SDKs with OIDC/SAML SSO and SCIM provisioning.

## Conventions

- Auth: bearer token + HMAC request signing; governed actions require digital signatures.
- Pagination: cursor-based (cursor.pageRequest / cursor.pageSize / cursor.currentPage; hasNext / hasPrevious).
- Errors: coded scheme E<NNN>-<NAME> (e.g. E900-INVALID_CURSOR_DATA); not RFC 9457.
- Versioning: URI path /api/rest/v1/.
- Webhooks: supported.
